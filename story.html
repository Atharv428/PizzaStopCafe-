Our Heart & Soul

Our Story — Made with Love, Served with Passion.

At Pizza Stop Alephata, we believe food is more than just a meal — it is an invitation to pause, share a smile, and celebrate the moments that matter most.

Our journey began with a heartfelt passion for authentic flavours and handcrafted food. Nestled along the bustling Pune-Nashik Highway in Alephata, we set out to create a warm, inviting destination where travelers taking a road break, local families gathering for celebrations, and friends catching up can all feel right at home.

Every pizza that leaves our kitchen starts with fresh dough kneaded daily, layered with rich house-blended sauces, 100% pure dairy mozzarella, and vibrant garden-fresh vegetables. We never cut corners, because we believe true culinary joy comes from honest ingredients, patient baking, and genuine attention to every detail.

“When you cook with real love, people don’t just taste the food — they feel the warmth behind it.”

Our vision is simple yet profound: to be Alephata’s favourite food destination — a place known not only for unforgettable pizzas, gourmet burgers, and creamy cold coffees, but for the smiles, memories, and warm hospitality that greet you every time you walk through our doors.
